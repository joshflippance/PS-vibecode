## What it tests

_Tie it back to the validation brief: which assumption does this prototype put in front of a user?_

Users will change their deeply ingrained habit of exporting data to spreadsheets (the 74% behaviour) and rely entirely on our automated dashboard if we surface a single, actionable recommendations  instead of just raw charts.

## Context injected (no placeholders)

- **Real user quotes on screen:** “I open it, see twelve charts, and have no idea which one I'm supposed to act on. So I close it.” / “The number I need is in there, but it takes six clicks and three filters to find it.”
- **Domain metrics on screen:** 60% Bounce rate: sessions under 15s with no interaction. / 12 Charts on the default landing view. / 6 clicks To reach the most-requested metric.74% users who export to a spreadsheet
